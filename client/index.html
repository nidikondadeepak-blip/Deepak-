📜 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 GOAL

Last ninja (or squad) standing wins. Everyone starts with 200 HP. The Akatsuki barrier shrinks — stay inside the ⛩ circle or burn!

🕹 CONTROLS

  • WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB throw weapon
  • 1 / 2 / 3 or wheel: Kunai / Shuriken / Paper Bomb
  • Q signature jutsu · Space jump (double-tap = double jump!)
  • Shift chakra sprint · ESC pause
  • Walk over 🍜 ramen to heal 60 HP

🌀 SIGNATURE JUTSU

  • Naruto — Rasengan: 70 dmg point-blank chakra sphere + huge knockback
  • Sasuke — Chidori: 14m lightning dash, 55 dmg + stun
  • Lee — Taijutsu Barrage: 5 flying kicks lock one enemy (60 dmg)
  • Hinata — Byakugan: see enemies through walls for 8s + speed
  • Sakura — Medical Ninjutsu: heal self 80 + squadmates 60

⚠ NINJA LAW

Kunai, shuriken and paper bombs only. Guns are forbidden in the Leaf Village. 🍃
